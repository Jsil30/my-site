About me

Craft meets
empathy.

I'm Jani — a UX/UI designer with a passion for creating digital experiences that are both beautiful and meaningful. I believe great design starts with understanding people: their goals, frustrations, and the context in which they interact with products.

My process blends rigorous user research with a refined visual sensibility. Whether I'm mapping out user journeys, crafting design systems, or polishing pixel-perfect interfaces, I bring the same care and intention to every detail.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring generative art, visiting design exhibitions, or mentoring aspiring designers.

How I work

My process

A structured yet flexible approach that keeps stakeholders aligned and users at the centre.

01

Discover

Research, stakeholder interviews, competitive analysis, and defining the problem space.

02

Define

Synthesise insights into personas, journey maps, and a clear design brief.

03

Design

Wireframes, visual design, interactive prototypes — iterated quickly and collaboratively.

04

Test

Usability testing with real users, A/B experiments, and data-informed refinements.

05

Deliver

Developer handoff with documented specs, assets, and ongoing design support.
